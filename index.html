Kora

A voice agentic co-rider for logistics riders. Riders talk instead of tapping, and Kora handles the next stop, the route and new order offers, then answers out loud so their eyes stay on the road.

  • Say “Hey Kora”. The wake word runs offline, on the phone.
  • Routes draw on an in-app map, so riders never get bounced to another app.
  • After a shift, it sums up the day: deliveries, targets and what went wrong.

About me.
